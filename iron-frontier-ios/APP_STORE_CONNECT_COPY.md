## English (U.S.)

**Subtitle**

Base Defense & Tactics

**Promotional Text**

Build a base, keep your supply lines open, and lead mobile units into enemy territory. Play the first chapter free.

**Description**

Build and defend a frontier base in a single-player strategy game for iPhone and iPad. Place your headquarters, supply power to your defenses, and keep your mining routes open as enemy waves approach. Train mobile units to guard supply lines, help your turrets hold the line, and push toward enemy bases.

- Open with three free missions in Redstone Quarry. Clear the chapter to play Endless and Challenge there.
- Practice building and commanding the full roster in the free Training Ground.
- Unlock the full game with a one-time purchase. Defend new terrain and take on 21 more campaign missions, with later maps available in Endless and Challenge as you progress.
- Keep a separate save for each mission and return to an interrupted battle on your device.

The full campaign spans six chapters and 24 missions. The game supports offline play, and the App Store handles purchases and restoration.

**Keywords**

tower defense,base building,strategy,offline,tactics,army,campaign,mining,outpost

## App Review 备注

Iron Frontier is a single-player offline strategy game for iPhone and iPad. The game opens directly to its lobby. To try the free campaign, start Chapter 1 and place your headquarters. The Training Ground is also available from the lobby.

The Full Game Unlock is a non-consumable in-app purchase. You can open purchase and restore controls from the lobby’s settings. The purchase is also offered from locked campaign content and after clearing Chapter 1. Chapter 1 and the Training Ground are free. Complete Chapter 1 to play the first map in Endless and Challenge. After the purchase, additional missions and mode maps open as the player advances.

Use Restore Purchases in the game’s settings to restore an existing entitlement. Campaign progress and battle saves are stored on the device. The game requires no developer account.
