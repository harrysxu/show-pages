Support

Get help

Email is the best way to reach Iron Frontier support. Include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and the level or screen where the issue occurred. For a gameplay problem, describe what happened just before the issue. You can include a screenshot with personal information removed.

Purchases

To recover access, open Settings in Iron Frontier and tap Restore Purchases while connected to the internet. Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Saved games

Campaign progress and saved battles are stored on your device. Use Apple’s device backup or transfer tools if you are moving to another device. Deleting the app removes its local data. Offloading the app through iPhone or iPad storage settings keeps its documents and data on that device.
