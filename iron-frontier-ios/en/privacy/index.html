Privacy Policy

Game data on your device

Iron Frontier stores campaign progress, saved battles, mode records, training presets and preferences on your device. It uses this data to resume play and apply your settings. The developer receives no gameplay data through the app. The game has no developer accounts, ads, third-party analytics or tracking.

Apple’s device backup or transfer features may include local app data, depending on your device settings. To remove the game’s local data from the device, choose Delete App in Settings. Manage any copies in device backups through the relevant Apple settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. Iron Frontier uses StoreKit to check the purchase status needed to unlock the full game and restore access. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under its privacy policy.
