Purchases and Refunds

Free content

Play the three missions of Chapter 1 and explore the Training Ground for free. Complete Chapter 1 to open Endless and Challenge on Redstone Quarry.

Full Game unlock

The Full Game unlock is a one-time App Store purchase. It grants access to 21 additional campaign missions across Chapters 2–6, the associated Endless and Challenge maps, and the bonus map earned after the final chapter. Missions and maps still unlock as you advance through the campaign.

The purchase screen shows the price provided by the App Store for your storefront. After your purchase has been verified on this device, you can play unlocked content offline.

Restore a purchase

Connect to the internet, sign in with the Apple Account used to buy the full game, and tap Restore Purchases in Iron Frontier Settings. A purchase that is awaiting approval becomes available after Apple completes it and the app verifies it.

Restoring the purchase restores access to the paid content. Your campaign progress and saved battles come from your local app data or from an Apple device backup or transfer.

Request a refund

For a refund, sign in to Apple’s Report a Problem service using the account used to buy the full game. Apple handles the request and determines eligibility. If Apple revokes the purchase, access to paid content ends after the game receives the updated purchase status.
