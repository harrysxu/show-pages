Frequently asked questions

Does the report send automatically? No. Review the preview, choose fields and select a recipient in the system share interface each time.

Does a negative month reduce a savings goal? No. A deficit only affects the suggested allocation; you adjust goal progress yourself.

Is Pro a subscription? No. It is a one-time CNY 18 purchase in mainland China, with no automatic renewal. See purchase and refund details.

Does Restore Purchases recover my ledger? No. It restores Pro access only. Import your separately saved JSON backup to restore ledger data. Export a backup before uninstalling.

Can the App verify my bank balance? No. It uses amounts you enter and does not connect to banks, WeChat or Alipay.
