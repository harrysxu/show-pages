Pro 一次性买断

中国大陆定价为人民币 18 元，通过 App Store 非消耗型应用内购买，一次付费永久解锁购买时列明的 Pro 权益。当前权益为薄荷主题、生活回顾月报模板和朋友版分享样式。其他地区按 App Store 显示当地价格和币种；付款前请核对系统购买确认页。

恢复购买与交易状态

重装应用或更换设备后，请使用原购买所用的 App Store 账户，在应用的 Pro 页面选择“恢复购买”。恢复的是 Pro 权益，不会恢复账目；账目请使用你保存的 JSON 备份单独导入。购买取消或待处理时不会解锁；商品或网络暂不可用时，可稍后重试。退款或交易被撤销后，对应 Pro 权益将按 App Store 交易状态取消，免费功能与账目仍可使用。

English

The product offers free core features and optional lifetime Pro. There are no subscriptions, automatic renewals or ads. Tracking, goals, basic monthly reports, family sharing and JSON backups remain free.

One-time Pro purchase

The price in mainland China is CNY 18, paid once through a non-consumable App Store in-app purchase. It permanently unlocks the Pro benefits listed on the purchase screen. Current benefits are the Mint theme, Life Recap report layout and Friends Report share style. Other regions use the local price and currency displayed by the App Store. Check the system purchase confirmation before paying.

This page describes a product in development; it does not mean the product is available for purchase. Free features and existing entries remain available without Pro. A purchase does not move goal funds or verify bank balances.

Restoring purchases and transaction status

After reinstalling or changing devices, use the App Store account that made the purchase and choose Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. This restores Pro access, not ledger data; import your saved JSON backup separately. Cancelled or pending purchases do not unlock Pro. Retry later if the product or network is unavailable. After a refund or transaction revocation, the corresponding Pro access is removed according to App Store transaction status; free features and ledger entries remain available.

Refunds

Apple handles App Store transactions. Request a refund through Apple’s Report a Problem or your Apple ID purchase history. Apple decides eligibility and processing under the applicable rules. The developer cannot access payment details or approve refunds on Apple’s behalf. For feature support, contact xuxiaolongyan@icloud.com.
