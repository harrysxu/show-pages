简体中文

本政策适用于“挣扎斩杀线”iOS/iPadOS 应用（下称“本应用”）。本应用由开发者徐晓龙提供。开发者把账目默认保留在你的设备上，不运营用于收集本应用账目数据的独立服务器。

本应用处理哪些信息

你可在设备上输入收入、支出、日期、分类、备注和储蓄目标。上述内容用于显示月度汇总和目标进度，保存在本应用的数据容器中。

分享和导出

你选择“分享”后，本应用会生成你预览过的月度摘要，并交给 iOS 系统分享界面。分享内容始终包括本月结余，并只加入你选择展示的其他汇总字段。接收目标应用可能保存、转发或继续处理该内容，其处理方式由该应用的政策决定。本应用无法控制你分享后的副本。你也可以主动把数据导出到 Files；导出文件可能含有个人收支信息，请妥善保管。

系统备份与 iCloud

本应用不会主动把账目传给开发者或第三方服务器。你的设备备份设置可能由 Apple 将应用数据包含在设备备份中，具体取决于你的 iCloud 或电脑备份设置。本应用不提供 CloudKit 多设备同步；如未来推出同步功能，会先更新本政策和应用内说明，并由你单独选择是否开启。

分析、广告与权限

本应用不含广告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK、账号注册或银行/支付平台连接。核心记账不需通讯录、位置或相机权限。仅当你选择将分享图片保存到照片图库时，应用会请求“仅添加照片”权限；拒绝后仍可使用系统分享。

应用内购买

Pro 由 Apple App Store 以非消耗型一次性购买处理。大陆区目标价为人民币 18 元；其他地区以结账页显示的当地价格为准。开发者不会收到你的银行卡或付款凭据。应用仅通过 StoreKit 查询已验证的 Pro 权益；没有订阅或自动续费。退款申请由 Apple 处理，详见购买与退款说明。

保留、查看与删除

你的账目保存在设备上。你可在应用内查看、编辑和删除单条记录；可以导出数据，并可使用“删除全部数据”清除本应用保存的账目。卸载应用会移除设备上的应用数据，但系统设备备份或你已分享/导出的副本可能仍存在，需要在对应位置单独删除。

儿童与联系

本应用面向一般个人用户，不会有意收集儿童个人信息。若你认为某项信息被不当处理，请联系开发者：xuxiaolongyan@icloud.com 或 15311074577。

本應用程式處理哪些資訊

本應用程式不會主動將帳目傳給開發者或第三方伺服器。Apple 可能依你的裝置備份設定，將應用程式資料納入 iCloud 或電腦備份。本應用程式不提供 CloudKit 多裝置同步；如未來推出同步功能，會先更新本政策與應用程式內說明，並由你另行選擇是否開啟。

帳目保存在裝置上。你可在應用程式內檢視、編輯和刪除單筆記錄，匯出資料，也可使用「刪除全部資料」清除本應用程式保存的帳目。解除安裝會移除裝置上的應用程式資料，但系統備份或已分享/匯出的副本可能仍存在，需在對應位置另行刪除。

English

This policy applies to Struggle Budget for iOS and iPadOS (the “App”), provided by developer Xu Xiaolong. Entries stay on your device by default. The developer does not operate a dedicated server to collect ledger data from the App.

Information the App handles

You may enter income, expenses, dates, categories, notes and savings goals. The App uses these entries to show monthly summaries and goal progress and stores them in its local data container.

Sharing and export

When you choose Share, the App creates the monthly summary you reviewed and passes it to the iOS system share interface. The report always includes the monthly net calculated from your entries and includes only the additional summary fields you select. A receiving app may store, forward or otherwise process the content under its own policy. The App cannot control copies after you share. You may also export data to Files; export files can contain personal financial information and should be protected.

System backups and iCloud

The App does not actively send ledger entries to the developer or a third-party server. Depending on your device backup settings, Apple may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup. The App does not provide CloudKit sync. If sync is introduced later, this policy and the in-app notice will be updated, and you will choose whether to enable it.

Analytics, ads and permissions

The App has no ad SDK, third-party analytics SDK, account registration, or bank/payment connection. Core tracking does not need Contacts, Location or Camera access. The App requests add-only Photos access only if you choose to save a report image to Photos; declining still allows system sharing.

In-app purchases

Apple’s App Store processes Pro as a one-time non-consumable purchase. The target price in mainland China is CNY 18; other regions use the local price shown at checkout. The developer does not receive your card or payment credentials. The App uses StoreKit only to check verified Pro entitlement; there are no subscriptions or automatic renewals. Apple handles refund requests. See purchase and refund details.

Retention, access and deletion

Entries are stored on your device. You can review, edit or delete individual entries, export data, or use Delete All Data in the App. Uninstalling removes the App’s device data, but system backups and copies you already shared or exported may remain and must be deleted separately.

Children and contact

The App is for general personal use and is not designed to knowingly collect children’s personal information. Contact the developer about privacy concerns at xuxiaolongyan@icloud.com or 15311074577. This policy will be reviewed against the actual release before launch.
