PaperFlow Privacy Policy

PaperFlow is a local-first PDF tool for iPhone and iPad. Import, scanning, OCR, editing and export are performed on the device by default.

Data handled on the device

The app may store PDFs, images, OCR text, thumbnails, annotations, signatures and workflow settings in the app container, Files locations selected by you, or a temporary App Group folder. PaperFlow does not require an account and does not upload your files to a PaperFlow server.

System permissions

Camera is used only when you scan. Photos are used only for items you choose. Files access is limited to files you select. PaperFlow does not use location, contacts or advertising tracking.

Purchases

Purchases and restore purchases are handled by Apple StoreKit. PaperFlow receives transaction status and product pricing for entitlement display and does not receive payment card details.

Contact

Use the support page to report a problem. Do not upload documents containing sensitive information.
