简体中文

静室 · 塔罗练习由开发者徐晓龙提供。我们以本地优先方式运行，不要求账户，不运营自有服务器。

我们处理的数据

你输入的问题、第一反应、带走的话、行动选择、抽到的牌、收藏和练习时间会存储在你的设备本地 SwiftData 数据库中。敏感问题在抽牌前由设备上的规则检查；被拦截的问题不会写入练习记录。当前版本不接入广告、分析 SDK 或远程推理接口。

设备端模型

当前版本随 App 内置 Qwen3-1.7B INT4 Core ML 模型和 tokenizer。只有你主动点击“生成情境提示”后，本次练习中的问题、自述、牌阵事实和本地牌义才会交由设备端模型处理；这些内容不会发送到开发者服务器。若模型加载失败或设备资源不足，App 保留本地牌义和规则模板解读。分享由你主动发起，系统分享面板会把你确认的内容交给所选 App。

保存、删除与安全

记录保存在设备上。你可以在 App 内编辑或删除记录；删除 App 或清除设备数据也会移除本机记录。当前版本没有云同步或账户恢复功能。请使用设备密码和系统安全功能保护设备。

儿童与政策变更

本产品不是为 13 岁以下儿童设计。若隐私实践发生实质变化，我们会更新此页面和生效日期。

隐私问题：xuxiaolongyan@icloud.com

English

Stillroom · Tarot Journal is provided by developer Xiaolong Xu. It is local-first, requires no account, and does not operate a proprietary server.

Data we handle

Your questions, first impressions, takeaways, selected actions, drawn cards, favorites, and practice dates are stored in a SwiftData database on your device. High-risk questions are checked locally before a draw; blocked questions are not saved as journal entries. The current version does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or remote inference APIs.

On-device model

This version bundles the Qwen3-1.7B INT4 Core ML model and tokenizer with the app. Only after you tap “Generate contextual reflection” are the current practice’s question, first impression, spread facts, and local meanings processed on device; this content is not sent to the developer’s server. If model loading fails or device resources are insufficient, the app keeps the local meanings and rule-based reflection available. Sharing is initiated by you; the system share sheet passes the content you confirm to the app you select.

Storage and deletion

Entries remain on your device. You can edit or delete them in the app; deleting the app or clearing device data also removes local entries. The current version has no cloud sync or account recovery. Protect your device with its passcode and system security features.

Children and changes

This product is not designed for children under 13. Material changes to these practices will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
