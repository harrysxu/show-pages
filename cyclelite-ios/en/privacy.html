Privacy policy

What CycleLite processes on your device, and how to export or delete your data.

Updated September 19, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

This policy applies to the current local-record version of CycleLite (期简). We design the app to minimize collection and transmission. No account is required. There are no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and no developer-operated server that receives cycle records.

Information processed and why

InformationPurposeStorage
Period dates and flowRecords, calendar, statistics, and estimated windowsLocal app storage
Symptoms and notes you enterDaily tracking and reviewLocal app storage
Cycle defaults, reminders, and lock preferencesApply your choicesLocal app storage
Random record identifiers and creation/update timestampsSaving, editing, and backup mergingLocal app storage

We do not sell these records, use them for advertising profiles, or send notes to AI services. The current version does not integrate with Apple Health or provide in-app iCloud sync.

Device security and authentication

Records are stored in the iOS app sandbox with system file protection. The optional privacy lock uses system authentication. The app receives the authentication result; it does not read or store face or fingerprint templates or your passcode. A privacy cover hides content in the app switcher when the app moves to the background.

These measures do not replace a secure device and passcode. System backups, including iCloud or computer backups you configure, may contain app data. This is separate from in-app sync and is governed by your settings and Apple’s policies.

Local notifications

Period window reminders are scheduled only after you enable them and grant system permission. There is no remote push service. The default lock screen message is a neutral health reminder. If you choose detailed mode, notifications include the estimated date. You can disable notifications in the app or in system settings.

Exports, imports, and other services

You can generate JSON or CSV files. Exports include the relevant records, and JSON also includes preferences. CycleLite does not add encryption to these files or automatically upload them. A storage provider, cloud service, or sharing recipient you choose may save or transfer them under its own privacy policy.

Imports are processed locally. The app validates a backup and merges or replaces records only after confirmation. If stored data cannot be read, the app may keep a local recovery copy to help prevent data loss.

Retention, correction, and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. You can view and correct them, export a backup, or delete all data in Settings. Full deletion includes local records, preferences, and app-managed recovery copies, and clears app reminders. It does not delete exported files, copies held by recipients, or system backups. Manage those separately.

The developer does not hold your cycle records and cannot restore deleted local content from a server. Keep your own backups of important records.

This website and system services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It adds no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses to serve the website; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Apple’s device backups, crash diagnostics, and other system services depend on your system settings and Apple’s policies.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, see Support and contact. Do not post period dates, symptoms, notes, or complete backups in public channels. We will update this policy and its revision date when features or data handling change.
