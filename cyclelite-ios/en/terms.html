Terms of use

The scope of CycleLite, your records, and the limits of health estimates.

Updated September 19, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

CycleLite (期简) provides personal cycle tracking, history review, and statistical estimates. Please read these terms and our privacy policy before using it.

Health information limits

The app is not a medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency service. Estimated windows are statistical estimates based on your input. They do not guarantee dates and must not be used for contraception, confirming ovulation, or determining pregnancy. Do not delay medical care based on the app. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms concern you.

Your records and backups

You choose what to record and can correct, export, or delete your entries. Record accuracy affects estimates. Save any needed backups before changing devices, uninstalling, replacing data during import, or deleting records. The developer has no server copy from which to restore your local records.

Responsible use

Only process information you are entitled to record, follow applicable law, and respect others’ privacy. Remove personal health information before contacting support.

Availability and changes

We aim to improve the app, but software can be affected by system versions, device conditions, and available storage. Changes to features, support, or terms will be reflected in version notes or on this page.

Your rights

These terms do not exclude or limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law. For help, visit Support.
