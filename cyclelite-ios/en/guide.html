From your first record to restoring a backup: everyday guidance for CycleLite.

01 / Start with your last period

During setup, add the start and optional end date of your last period, or skip this step. Reminders are off by default.

  1. On Today, tap Start period to record today as the start date.
  2. When your period ends, tap End period to record today as the end date.
  3. To correct dates, open a recent record on Today, or select a recorded day in Calendar and tap Edit.
  4. Log today lets you add optional flow, symptoms, and notes as a separate daily entry.

Dates cannot be in the future, and the end cannot precede the start. If an edit overlaps another period, you must confirm a merge. Merging retains symptoms and distinct notes.

02 / Understand your rhythm

Calendar distinguishes recorded periods from estimated windows and marks days with entries. Trends shows recorded cycle lengths and the duration of completed periods.

Cycle length is the interval between consecutive start dates. Period length includes both the start and end days. Defaults are used when history is limited. Tap the estimate on Today to see the cycles used, typical length, and uncertainty.

Cycles naturally vary. An estimated window is not a certain date. Do not use it for contraception, confirming ovulation, determining pregnancy, or diagnosis. If symptoms concern you, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

03 / Consistent language and dates

CycleLite supports English and Simplified Chinese. Open iPhone Settings → Apps → CycleLite → Language to choose your app language. You can also use Settings → App language inside CycleLite to open system settings.

If the language option is missing, first add another supported language under Settings → General → Language & Region, then return to CycleLite’s settings.

Date text, month and weekday names, and date pickers use the app language. Date order and the first day of the week also reflect your region. The same day may appear as “August 8, 2026”, “8 August 2026”, or “2026年8月8日”. Your own notes are never translated when you change languages.

04 / Export and restore

  • JSON backup: Choose Settings → Export JSON backup to save records and preferences for restoration or migration.
  • CSV data: Designed for reading in spreadsheet software. CSV cannot be imported into the app. Dates use yyyy-MM-dd; column names and flow codes stay stable. Symptom names use the app language at export.
  • Import: Choose a CycleLite JSON backup no larger than 10 MB, review the record counts, choose merge or replace, and confirm.
  • Merge: Keeps other local records and uses the backup version for matching record identifiers. Overlapping periods or different entries on the same day block the import.
  • Replace: Replaces local records with backup records. Export your current data first. Restoring preferences is a separate option.

CycleLite does not add encryption to exported JSON or CSV files. They may contain personal health information. Save them somewhere you trust. If you choose a cloud provider, uploading depends on that provider and your settings.

05 / Privacy lock and reminders

The optional privacy lock uses system authentication: Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, depending on your device setup. Authentication is required to enable or disable it.

Local reminders can be scheduled 1, 2, 3, or 5 days before the estimated window. Private mode only shows “You have a health reminder”. Detailed mode includes the estimated date. System notification permission is also required. Turning reminders off cancels pending reminders.

Deleting an individual record requires confirmation. Settings → Delete all data removes local records, preferences, app-managed recovery copies, and app reminders. Manage exported files and system backups separately.
