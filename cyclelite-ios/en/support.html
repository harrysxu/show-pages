CycleLite / Documentation

Support & common questions

Help with languages, dates, backups, reminders, and the privacy lock.

Updated September 19, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Why do dates look different from the app language?

The internationalization update makes date text and system date pickers use the same app language. Use a version that includes this update and check Settings → Apps → CycleLite → Language. Your device region still affects date order and the first day of the week. See the language guide.

Why is there no estimated window?

Add a period first. With limited history, CycleLite uses your default cycle length and labels the estimate accordingly. Estimates update as you record more history; they are not certain outcomes.

Why can’t I import my backup?

Choose a CycleLite JSON backup no larger than 10 MB, not a CSV file. Unsupported versions, invalid dates, overlapping periods, or different entries on the same day may block imports. Resolve conflicts first. If you choose replacement, export your local data beforehand.

Why didn’t a reminder appear?

Check the in-app reminder switch, iPhone notification permissions, Focus, and Scheduled Summary. CycleLite only schedules reminders whose dates have not passed. It does not send daily logging prompts.

What if privacy lock authentication fails?

Make sure a device passcode or biometric authentication is configured. Records stay locked if authentication fails or is cancelled; tap Unlock to try again. There is no bypass for system authentication. Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting step without a backup.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a JSON backup from the old device, transfer it securely, and import it on the new one. The current version has no in-app iCloud sync or Apple Health import. Whether system device restoration includes app data depends on your backup settings.
