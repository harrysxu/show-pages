Version & language support

Current functionality and the English and Simplified Chinese localization update.

Updated September 19, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Current version · 1.0.0

These notes describe the current development version. They do not indicate App Store availability.

  • Editable periods and daily entries with optional flow, symptoms, and notes.
  • Estimated windows, a monthly calendar, and cycle trends.
  • Local reminders, optional device authentication, and a background privacy cover.
  • JSON backup and merge/replace import, CSV export, and local data deletion.

Internationalization update

English and Simplified Chinese cover onboarding, Today, Calendar, Trends, Settings, daily entry, period editing, estimate explanations, About, import preview, confirmations, errors, notifications, and accessibility labels.

  • Date pickers and displayed dates share the app language.
  • Date order and the first weekday reflect your device region.
  • English day and record counts use singular and plural forms.
  • Changing language preserves your notes and the dates and fields in JSON backups.

Verification scope

31 unit tests and 12 UI tests passed across the initial run and focused retest after a fix. Coverage includes both languages, date pickers, US and UK formats, plurals, and stable backup dates. An unsigned release archive succeeded, and the demo simulator retained its 10 records unchanged after updating. Real-device notifications, authentication, and the largest accessibility text sizes still require release acceptance testing.

Current scope

The current version focuses on local records. Apple Health, in-app iCloud sync, and widgets are not included. This page describes implemented features; future functionality depends on actual releases.
