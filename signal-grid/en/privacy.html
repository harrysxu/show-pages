Privacy Policy

Updated November 25, 2026

This policy applies to Hull Circuit for iPhone and iPad.

Game data

The game runs offline and collects no personal information. It saves your level progress, in-progress circuit layouts, and sound settings on your device so you can resume playing.

In-app purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. The game uses the purchase status returned by Apple to unlock chapters. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Retention and deletion

The game keeps your progress and settings on your device. Choose Delete App on your iPhone or iPad to remove the local game data. If you back up your device, any copies in those backups are managed through your device backup settings.

Support emails

When you email us, we receive your email address and any details you choose to include. We use this information to respond to and resolve your request and keep it for as long as needed to handle the request and any follow-up. You can ask us to delete your support records using the address below.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub handles website access data as described in its privacy statement. GitHub Privacy Statement
