How do I repair a circuit?

Tap a movable connector to rotate it until its ports line up with neighboring ports. A connected circuit carries power to the required devices and the exit. Use the in-game hint if you get stuck.

How do I resume my game?

The game automatically saves your progress on your device. Use the resume option in the energy hub to return to the next room that needs repair.

Which bays are free?

Chapter 1 includes 35 free bays. A one-time purchase unlocks the 65 bays in Life Support and Flight Control as you reach them in the story.

How do I restore a purchase?

If the chapters are locked, tap the shopping bag in the Energy Center and choose Restore Purchases. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account you used to buy the unlock. Restoring the purchase restores access to the chapters. Your puzzle progress is saved separately on your device.
