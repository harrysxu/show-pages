How do I repair a circuit?

Tap a movable connector to rotate it until its ports line up with neighboring ports. A connected circuit carries power to the required devices and the exit. Use the in-game hint if you get stuck.

How do I resume my game?

The game automatically saves your progress on your device. Use the resume option in the energy hub to return to the next room that needs repair.
