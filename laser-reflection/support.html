Support

For questions or feedback about Relight the Station, email xuxiaolongyan@icloud.com.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version, the affected room or level, and the steps that led to the issue.

Restore a purchase

In the Power Hub, open the More menu and choose Unlock Chapters 2 & 3 or Purchases. Tap Restore Purchases and use the Apple Account that originally bought the game unlock.

Privacy policy

技术支持

如有《激光反射》的使用问题或反馈，请发送邮件至 xuxiaolongyan@icloud.com。

反馈游戏问题时，请附上设备型号、iOS 或 iPadOS 版本、游戏版本、遇到问题的舱室或关卡，以及能够重现问题的操作步骤。

恢复购买

在能源中心打开「更多」菜单，选择「解锁第二、三章」或「购买记录」，再点按「恢复购买」。请使用原来购买解锁项目的 Apple 账户。
