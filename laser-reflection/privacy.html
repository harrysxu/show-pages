Privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026

This policy describes how Relight the Station for iPhone and iPad handles information.

Game data

Puzzles can be played offline. Your progress and sound settings are saved on your device so you can continue where you left off. The developer collects no personal information through the game.

App Store purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time purchase and handles account and payment information under its privacy policy. The game uses Apple StoreKit to check on your device whether the later chapters are unlocked. Buying or restoring a purchase may require an internet connection.

Deleting local data

Delete the game from your device to remove its local data. Copies may remain in system backups, depending on your backup settings.

Support and privacy requests

If you email support, your email address and the details you share are used to respond to your request. Use the support contact for privacy questions or to request deletion of your support correspondence.

隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 25 日

本政策说明适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 的《激光反射》如何处理信息。

游戏数据

关卡可离线游玩。关卡进度和声音设置保存在你的设备上，方便下次继续。开发者不会通过游戏收集个人信息。

App Store 购买

可选的一次性内购由 Apple 处理。账户与付款信息按 Apple 的隐私政策处理。游戏使用 Apple StoreKit 在设备上检查后续章节的解锁状态。购买或恢复购买时可能需要联网。

清除本地数据

从设备删除游戏即可清除游戏的本地数据。根据设备的备份设置，系统备份中可能保留副本。

技术支持与隐私请求

如果你通过邮件联系技术支持，我们会使用你的邮箱地址和你提供的信息处理请求。若有隐私问题或需要删除支持邮件记录，可通过同一渠道联系。
