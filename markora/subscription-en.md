# Markora Pro subscriptions, renewal, and refunds

**Effective date: September 18, 2026**

## What Pro includes

Markora Pro unlocks:

- **Scan to Markdown**: capture with the camera or import images/PDFs, recognize text on-device, and create editable Markdown;
- **Advanced Markdown blocks**: Mermaid diagrams and LaTeX formulas;
- **HTML and PDF export**: export the current document as HTML or PDF.

Basic Markdown editing and local document handling may be available without a subscription. The in-app paywall and current version control the final feature set.

## Plans and price

The current subscription group offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable plans:

| Plan | App Store product ID | Period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly Pro | `com.xiaolongxu.NoliaOne.pro.monthly` | Monthly |
| Yearly Pro | `com.xiaolongxu.NoliaOne.pro.yearly` | Yearly |

Apple displays the price, currency, taxes, and available offers for your storefront at purchase. Use the Apple purchase screen as the source of truth; this page intentionally does not fix an amount.

## Auto-renewal and cancellation

Apple automatically renews the subscription unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it at **Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions**, or use the App’s Manage Subscription action.

After cancellation, access usually continues until the end of the paid period; Apple’s status is authoritative.

## Restore purchases

On a device using the same Apple Account, choose Restore Purchases in the subscription screen. The App asks Apple for the current entitlement and does not charge you again.

## Refunds, billing, and taxes

Apple handles payment, billing, taxes, refunds, and disputes. Use [Apple’s report-a-problem page](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) or Apple Support to request a refund. Markora cannot issue a direct refund, change a bill, or access complete payment details.

## Family sharing and trials

The current product configuration does not offer Family Sharing. Trials, introductory offers, and promotions are available only if Apple shows them for your storefront.

## Support

If access does not appear after purchase, confirm the Apple Account used for the purchase and tap Restore Purchases. For further help, use the [Support page](support-en.md). Do not post receipts, order numbers, or payment information publicly.
