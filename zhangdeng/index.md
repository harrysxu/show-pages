# 掌灯：永昌堡守夜记

## 简体中文

《掌灯》是为 2026 开源游戏开发大赛「守·望」主题创作的游戏。玩家在永昌堡东门守夜，举灯辨认城墙下的人，再收灯出刀、推梯或开门接人。

### 史料与演出

1558 年，王沛、王德抗倭殉难。永昌堡同年开筑，1559 年落成。东门瓮城的外门朝南，敌台曾夜间举火联络。宁村汤和出巡以农历七月十五为正日，当地有掌灯归来和拼字龙灯的习俗。拼字龙灯的来历有多种口传说法。

本作基于史料创作。守夜人、一夜五更的事件、细作和英灵归城属于虚构。横版画面简化了瓮城门的方位，尾声灯阵是对拼字龙灯的演出设计。

## 繁體中文

《掌燈》是為 2026 開源遊戲開發大賽「守·望」主題創作的遊戲。玩家在永昌堡東門守夜，舉燈辨認城牆下的人，再收燈出刀、推梯或開門接人。

### 史料與演出

1558 年，王沛、王德抗倭殉難。永昌堡同年開築，1559 年落成。東門甕城的外門朝南，敵臺曾在夜間舉火聯絡。寧村湯和出巡以農曆七月十五為正日，當地有掌燈歸來和拼字龍燈的習俗。拼字龍燈的來歷有多種口傳說法。

本作依據史料創作。守夜人、一夜五更的事件、細作和英靈歸城屬於虛構。橫版畫面簡化了甕城門的方位，尾聲燈陣是對拼字龍燈的演出設計。

## English

*Keep the Light: A Night on Yongchang Fort* is a game made for the 2026 Open Source Game Development Competition. You guard the fort’s east gate, raise your lantern to see who is below, then lower it to defend the wall or let people in.

### History and the game

Wang Pei and Wang De died fighting raiders in 1558. The villagers began building Yongchang Fort that year and finished it in 1559. The outer gate of the east barbican faces south. The fort’s towers used signal fires at night. In Ningcun, the main day of the Tang He procession is the fifteenth day of the seventh lunar month. The festivities include a return by lantern light and dragon lanterns arranged to form characters. Oral accounts tell several stories about the origin of these lanterns.

The game is a work of fiction based on historical records. The watchman, events of the five watches, spies and returning spirits are fictional. The side view simplifies the barbican layout, and the epilogue’s display is an interpretation of the dragon lantern tradition.

### Privacy and support

Language and audio preferences and playtest logs are stored on the device, or in the browser’s site storage for the web version. The log records game events and can be copied from the results screen. The web host handles requests to load the game.
